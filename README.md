# Trader Performance & Market Sentiment
## Data Science Assignment Submission

**Dataset:** Hyperliquid Historical Trade Data + Bitcoin Fear & Greed Index[cite: 2]
**Period:** May 2023 – May 2025[cite: 2]
**Objective:** Explore the relationship between market sentiment and trader execution performance[cite: 2].

---

### Executive Summary

This analysis cross-references over 184,000 trade executions with daily Bitcoin sentiment classifications[cite: 2]. The data empirically demonstrates that **Fear market conditions yield the highest gross profitability and peak win rates** — contradicting standard retail trading assumptions that calm or bullish markets are the most profitable[cite: 2].

### Key Findings

* **The Fear Advantage:** Fear conditions produced the highest total realized PnL ($6.69M) and the highest win rate (86.12%) of any sentiment regime[cite: 2]. 
* **Extreme Greed Degrades Performance:** During Extreme Greed, average PnL per trade collapses to just $40.85 — the lowest of any regime — while win rates also compress (78.76%)[cite: 2]. 
* **Contrarian Selling into Greed:** In standard Greed conditions, Sell-tagged trades and closed shorts captured outsized PnL with strong win rates[cite: 2]. 
* **Adaptive Traders Outperform Rigid Ones:** Top traders generated massive profits in Fear but some lost heavily in Greed due to rigid systems blind to regime shifts[cite: 2]. 

### Recommended Playbook
* **Extreme Fear:** Accumulate longs aggressively; widen take-profit targets[cite: 2].
* **Fear:** Maximize allocation; both long and short opportunities available[cite: 2].
* **Neutral:** Reduce size; avoid overtrading; wait for a clear regime[cite: 2].
* **Greed:** Begin taking profits; bias toward contrarian short entries[cite: 2].
* **Extreme Greed:** Minimise exposure; tighten stops; avoid new long entries[cite: 2].
